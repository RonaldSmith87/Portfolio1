Create a post responding to the following:

### 1. Strengths

- What concepts or skills do you feel you’ve **excelled at** so far?
    

---

### 2. Challenges

- What have you **struggled with the most**?
    

---

### 3. Areas for Improvement

Identify **three areas** you want to improve during this course.

For each area:

- Describe the skill or concept
    
- Explain why you chose it
    

---

### 4. Learning Goals [(SMART Goals)](https://www.smartsheet.com/blog/essential-guide-writing-smart-goals)

For each of your three areas, create a [**clear learning goal**.](https://www.smartsheet.com/blog/essential-guide-writing-smart-goals)

Your goals should:

- be specific
    
- be measurable
    
- show how you will know you’ve improved
    

Example:

> “Improve my understanding of pointers by successfully implementing dynamic memory in my project without errors.”
